# Router Research Integration Acceptance

Required behaviors:
1. External evidence acquisition/synthesis routes to Research.
2. Research remains distinct from Evaluate/adoption decisions.
3. Specific system-state/root-cause work remains Investigate.
4. Target-machine prerequisite questions remain Readiness.
5. Specific factual/acceptance claims can route to Verify.
6. Research does not authorize install, clone, execute, purchase, adoption, or protected-state mutation.
7. Security-sensitive findings can route Research -> Security.
8. Planned skills remain unroutable.
9. Meaningful research milestones can route to Closeout.
10. Smallest sufficient route remains preferred.

Fail if Research is used as automatic adoption authority or as a substitute for Investigate, Readiness, Verify, Security, or Release.
