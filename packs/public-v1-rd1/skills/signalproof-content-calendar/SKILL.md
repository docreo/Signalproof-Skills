## Workflow

1. Define objective, audience, platforms, period, sustainable cadence, claims, assets, accessibility, and publishing authority.

2. Inventory verified source facts and authorized media. Flag expired or unsupported claims.

3. Build campaign themes and slots with a reason for each post rather than filling a calendar for its own sake.

4. Draft platform-specific hooks, formats, calls to action, links, asset briefs, and alternative text.

5. Review accuracy, repetition, rights, tone, links, and date relevance; preserve draft/review/approved distinctions.

6. Schedule or publish only under explicit scope with an available connector. Read back account, timing, content, and provider identity; otherwise mark a draft handoff.

## Result and verification

Calendar slots and objectives; draft briefs; source/asset rights state; editorial disposition; verified schedule or draft handoff.

Return a scoped PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL, BLOCKED, or UNVERIFIED result when a verification claim is made. Report unavailable checks explicitly. Do not claim runtime-host validation, deployment, or delivery merely because this workflow produced a draft.
