## Workflow

1. Detect candidate cuts and verify them visually; scene-score thresholds are heuristics rather than a final shot list.

2. Inspect representative and moving frames within each shot for face, hands, props, text, and usable negative space.

3. Choose card zones per shot and target aspect ratio; preserve meaningful source content and current platform safe regions.

4. Place contrast treatments behind lettering when needed without obscuring the subject or essential action.

5. Keep a readable title or thought across a cut when necessary; shot boundaries need not truncate the meaning.

6. Composite overlays over actual frames and inspect assembled playback, not just isolated overlay screenshots.
