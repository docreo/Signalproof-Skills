## Optional Signalproof owner profile

Apply these owner-designated VideoCraft defaults to authorized Signalproof productions unless overridden by the current request. They describe styling, not permission to use marks or a claim that assets are included.

- Brand masters: red `#990000`, gold `#FFCC00`; screen accent red `#CE1126`. Avoid orange. Obtain the genuine approved logo from the owner/current authorized brand source; do not reconstruct it from a composite.
- Preserve spellings including `Signalproof` and `HAMM`. Load additional names/canon from the job, not a public biography or invented story bible.
- Prefer word-timed gold drawings/highlights and red accents, unobstructed faces. Approved designs may include a logo bug, meter, and signal line.
- Preserve supplied finished audio. Where music is authorized, use an instrumental-only opening ending before sung words; the speaker supplies the first words heard.
- Budget one major sub-bass impact for rants/concept shorts or two for explainers/mini lessons. Use lighter effects elsewhere, only with permission for additions.
- Leave profanity in audio by default; censor display captions and `.srt`. Bleep only on request over confirmed spans.
- Use the approved stamp/closing. When a full-wrap clip counterpart is authorized under this profile, deliver requested cut and wrapped variant with distinct names/durations. A request for only one unwrapped export overrides this default.
- Standard closing wording, if requested: “BLOCK OUT THE NOISE.” / “FIND THE TRUE SIGNAL.” / “TAKE IN PROVEN KNOWLEDGE.” / “SEND A CLEAR SIGNAL.”, then “GET STARTED. GET SIGNALPROOF.” Use approved design/timing rather than inventing a replica.
- For independently styled fiction/music worlds, use supplied art direction instead of this corporate layer.
- Use the configured owner destination and preview limits from the job. No personal drive, song, infrastructure, or private media is included here.

If a necessary asset or decision is unavailable, record the dependency and continue independent planning. Do not fabricate missing assets or mark the blocked gate PASS.
