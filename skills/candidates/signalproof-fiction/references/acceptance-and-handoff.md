# Chapter Remaster Acceptance and Handoff

## Required working artifacts

- Immutable original or versioned source, identified unambiguously.
- Remastered working chapter, complete from title through final sentence.
- Visible change record, documenting exact edits or paragraph-level replacements.
- Structural and continuity audit with material exceptions.
- Proposed author decisions separate from automatic accepted edits.

## Verification checklist

1. Confirm source identity and that the latest chapter was actually read.
2. Compare complete original and revision, not merely the first pages.
3. Count source and revised words with the same counting method. Treat count as measurement, never a target.
4. Confirm no accidental duplicate/deleted paragraphs, missing scenes, or lost chapter ending.
5. Verify dates, historical setting, rank, shipboard logistics, character relationships, laws, powers, clues, and reveal order against controlling story sources.
6. Verify dialogue speaker distribution using private voice cards; allow characterization to evolve where story requires it.
7. Check prohibited punctuation and recurring rhetorical habits. Report counts or examples only when measured.
8. Check additions for dramatic function and cuts for lost causal information.
9. Explicitly record earlier author corrections restored or preserved.
10. Supply a concise AI-use log: source, scope, changed categories, restorations, new material, unresolved author choices, actual verification performed.

## Status language

- **Draft for author review:** revised text exists; pending author acceptance.
- **Author accepted:** owner approved this chapter revision, with any recorded exceptions.
- **Canon-locked:** owner explicitly designated the accepted version canonical.
- **Partial edit:** only a defined subset was revised; say so.
- **Whole-book remaster:** every chapter has passed its chapter-level gate and the complete manuscript has passed a book-level continuity read.

## Handoff record

Use a short record containing chapter version, scene chain, total changed paragraphs, source/revised word counts, open decisions, accepted voice cards, continuity conflicts, next chapter boundary, and canonical references. Do not imply a PR candidate has been promoted into Active public governance before acceptance/merge.
