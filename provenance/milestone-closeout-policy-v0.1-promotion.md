- `signalproof` -> 0.1.1 Active
- `signalproof-router` -> 0.1.1 Active

## Accepted behavior

- meaningful milestones require `signalproof-closeout` before the next development phase unless the human owner explicitly records a justified deferral;
- ordinary micro-commits and intra-phase edits do not require full milestone closeout;
- explicit owner deferral remains Design Authority and does not falsely become completed closeout;
- canonical Build Ledger mutation authority remains separate from routing/closeout capability.

1. meaningful milestone -> closeout required;
2. ordinary intra-phase work -> no forced closeout;
3. explicit owner deferral -> preserved without false completion claim.

No root authority, protected-state, evidence, recovery, public/private, or active-skill-only routing boundary was weakened.
