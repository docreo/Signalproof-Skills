- **D** distinct operating responsibility;
- **G** current-library gap after overlap review;
- **V** operational/intellectual/commercial/creative value;
- **T** falsifiable testability.

## Existing-library overlap findings

### `signalproof-workflow-mine` vs `signalproof-learn`

Distinct gap confirmed.

- Learn begins from completed evidence-backed work and extracts the smallest reusable mechanism.
- Workflow Mine begins from a historical corpus, detects repeated implicit workflows, then hands justified patterns into Learn/governance.

Disposition: separate Candidate is justified.

### `signalproof-distill`

Potential overlap: Cut Chase, Document, Grill, Research.

Gap: Cut Chase compresses established material; Distill reconstructs structure from unstructured or partially formed human input while preserving intent and uncertainty. This is a different transformation.

Disposition: strong Candidate, but acceptance must prove it does not become generic summarization.

### `signalproof-clarity`

Potential overlap: Evaluate, Investigate, Grill.

Gap: a Signalproof-native diagnostic specifically mapping intended signal, observed signal, noise, proof, gap/root distortion, prescription, and first observable action is not currently owned by those capabilities.

Disposition: strong Candidate and likely core Signalproof IP.

### `signalproof-workflow-audit`

Potential overlap: Investigate, Plan, Evaluate, Readiness.

Gap: repeatable business/process mapping through ownership, handoffs, leaks, bottlenecks, proof, systemization, and automation-last is a coherent operating diagnosis with commercial and organizational use.

Disposition: strongest immediate Candidate after Workflow Mine.

### `signalproof-content`

Potential overlap: Document, Design, Build Capsule.

Gap: source-to-audience content strategy/production has a different transformation and output contract.

Decomposition decision: begin with one principal Content Candidate. Treat repurpose, publishing-package, brand-voice, and visual packaging as modes/configuration until independent routing/evolution/testing justify specialists.

### `signalproof-academic`

Potential overlap: Research and Document.

Gap: scholarly manuscript methodology adds thesis/authority structure, counterargument, quotation/paraphrase discipline, citation integrity, academic-integrity checks, and manuscript-level scholarly acceptance.

Disposition: strong Candidate consuming Research rather than duplicating it.

### `signalproof-learning-design`

Potential overlap: Teach, Plan, Document.

Gap: Teach executes instruction; Learning Design architects outcomes, sequence, practice, assessment, feedback, competence proof, and transfer.

Disposition: strong Candidate. Keep Assessment as a future specialist until complexity justifies split.

### `signalproof-canon`

Potential overlap: Knowledge, Document, Review.

Gap: source hierarchy, canon facts, reveal state, character knowledge, protected mystery, continuity, and locked creative material form a distinct creative-governance capability.

Disposition: strong Candidate. Manuscript Review and Narrative Adapt should initially consume Canon rather than compete with it.

### `signalproof-offer-architect`

Potential overlap: Evaluate, Research, Plan.

Gap: constructing a commercial system from buyer/problem/outcome/deliverable/proof/entry/core/premium/price is constructive architecture, not evaluation of an existing option.

Disposition: strong Candidate; pricing/economic acceptance needs careful evidence boundaries.

### `signalproof-agent-blueprint`

Potential overlap: Plan, Governor authority model, Agent Action Loop, Build.

Gap may exist for a standardized specialized-AI-role specification covering mission, authority/non-authority, inputs/outputs, tools, memory need, data boundary, escalation, human handoff, acceptance, and fail conditions.

Disposition: design review before Candidate implementation. It must not become an autonomous-agent builder or duplicate Governor policy.

## Negative learning

The main risk in retroactive Skill mining is **taxonomy inflation**: confusing repeated subject matter, formatting, or named outputs with independently routeable capability. This first assessment therefore deliberately reduces sixteen proposed principal Skills to nine strong domain/foundational candidates, one design-review candidate, and six held modes/specialists.
