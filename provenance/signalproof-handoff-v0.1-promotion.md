# signalproof-handoff V0.1 Promotion Evidence

## Decision

Promote `signalproof-handoff` to `0.1.0 / Active public baseline` after the governed candidate scenario, owner approval, repository consistency validation, and pull-request workflow complete.

## Acceptance basis

The candidate scenario modeled a long evidence-heavy engineering conversation at the boundary between a completed milestone and a new major phase.

Result:

- 15/15 required handoff behaviors PASS;
- 0 fail conditions;
- no fabricated context percentage;
- GREEN / YELLOW / RED Context Pressure established;
- exact-meter thresholds made conditional on a trustworthy real platform meter;
- protected state, failure history, repository state, and Build Ledger boundaries preserved;
- exact next action and receiving-chat boot path required;
- chat handoff kept distinct from product milestone closeout;
- public/private boundary preserved.

## Governance lesson

Conversation continuity is an operational artifact, not a memory assumption. A project may need a handoff even when the underlying phase is still open, and a completed product milestone may still require a separate conversation-boundary transfer before the next major phase.

## Provenance

The Signalproof-native capability grows from repeated Signalproof work-chat handoffs and the prior Chat-Boundary Handoff Protocol. The general concept of resumable agent handoff was also studied through Matt Pocock's public handoff work under Signalproof's existing upstream-learning and provenance framework.

## Scope

This promotion adds conversation-boundary continuity governance. It does not create canonical Build Ledger mutation authority, release authority, destructive authority, or permission to publish private operational handoffs.
