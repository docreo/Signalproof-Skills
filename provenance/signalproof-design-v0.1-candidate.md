## Origin

Candidate created from an owner-approved Signalproof-wide design policy decision after Competition Radar C2 reached automated and visual acceptance.

The policy decision established that future Signalproof software candidates should begin at `V1 RD1`, substantial products should provide owner-facing dashboards plus technical/evidence depth, products should use replaceable logo/icon asset slots, and health/readiness communication should remain evidence-backed.

## Candidate design principles

- V1 RD1 prospective starting rule for newly originated Signalproof software candidates.
- No retroactive renumbering of existing product histories.
- Replaceable brand-asset slots with a default Signalproof identity.
- Branding and layout treated as separate governance concerns.
- Operational View and Technical/Evidence View for substantial products.
- Dashboard requirement proportional to state/workflow complexity.
- Unknown state never silently treated as healthy.
- Large-application layout discipline may borrow Greenlight-style information architecture without copying Greenlight product content or semantics.
- Major redesigns preserve the accepted prior baseline and use concept/mockup plus distinct visual/runtime acceptance gates.
- Lifecycle state and strategic disposition remain separate when both matter.

## Canonical artwork boundary

The public Skills repository defines how products consume brand assets but is not the source of truth for canonical production artwork. Signalproof master brand assets should remain in a controlled brand-asset source with provenance/version/hash references as appropriate.

## Validation requirement

Before promotion to Active:

1. validate against Competition Radar C3 Intelligence Dashboard & Technology Disposition;
2. validate against a materially different Signalproof product;
3. preserve product-specific semantics in both cases;
4. prove dashboard/health/technical-view claims are tied to real state rather than presentation-only placeholders;
5. obtain explicit owner approval;
6. only then update the active registry/router and promotion provenance.
