# Human + AI Maturity

The **Human + AI Maturity Model**, often expressed through **GO HAMM**, is a Signalproof framework for thinking about AI readiness as a progression of human capability, operating discipline, and controlled automation rather than a race to install more tools.

## Why maturity matters

AI systems can become more capable while the human or organization operating them remains unclear about goals, data, authority, risk, workflow, or accountability.

That gap is dangerous because technical capability can amplify both good and bad operating decisions.

Human + AI maturity asks whether the surrounding human system is ready for the level of AI capability being introduced.

## A practical progression

The progression can be summarized as:

1. **Clarity** — understand the goal, current state, constraints, and real problem.
2. **Readiness** — establish prerequisites, access, ownership, boundaries, and recovery.
3. **Learning** — use AI to increase understanding and capability without silently outsourcing judgment.
4. **Correction** — repair weak workflows, missing information, broken systems, and governance gaps.
5. **Integration** — connect tools and workflows deliberately.
6. **Automation** — automate only after the process, authority, evidence, and failure handling are understood.
7. **Governed scale** — expand AI capability without losing visibility, accountability, or human control.

## What maturity is not

Maturity is not measured simply by:

- how many AI subscriptions a person owns;
- how many agents are running;
- whether a company uses the newest model;
- how much work is automated;
- how little a human participates.

A highly automated system with unclear authority, weak evidence, poor recovery, and no meaningful human oversight can be less mature than a simpler system that is well understood and governed.

## Human role

Signalproof treats the human as an active operating authority, not a ceremonial approval button.

Meaningful human control requires enough visibility and competence to understand material actions, establish boundaries, challenge results, stop execution, and recover from failure.

## AI role

AI can contribute speed, synthesis, pattern recognition, generation, translation, analysis, orchestration, and automation. Its role should expand as the surrounding operating system proves that it can safely support greater capability.

The core idea is simple:

> **Do not automate confusion. Mature the human-AI system first.**
