# Products and Research

Signalproof is developing as an ecosystem rather than a single application. Public descriptions should distinguish between concepts, research programs, development candidates, validated internal tools, and released products.

## Public foundation

### Signalproof Skills

The public **Signalproof Skills** repository contains governed operating disciplines for AI-assisted research, evaluation, planning, design, readiness, implementation, debugging, verification, review, security, recovery, release, documentation, handoff, learning, and closeout.

It is the clearest public technical expression of the Signalproof operating philosophy today.

## Software directions

Signalproof research and development includes work in areas such as:

- local-first AI infrastructure and runtime management;
- system and AI readiness assessment;
- governed capability intake and tool evaluation;
- software build and recovery evidence;
- media and creative production systems;
- knowledge transformation and technical-information systems;
- opportunity discovery and analysis;
- competitive and market intelligence;
- business memory, decision continuity, and human-controlled agent workflows.

Specific repositories may remain private while products are in development, security review, validation, licensing review, or pre-release testing.

## Knowledge transformation

Signalproof is researching governed transformation of source knowledge into structured, reusable outputs. This includes technical information, learning content, reference materials, and machine-usable knowledge structures.

The central concern is not simply conversion. It is preservation of provenance, source authority, traceability, human review, and appropriate output boundaries.

## Opportunity intelligence

Signalproof is developing methods for discovering, normalizing, analyzing, scoring, and tracking public opportunities such as grants, contracts, research solicitations, procurement notices, and related programs.

The intended value is decision support: understanding requirements, capability fit, build-versus-partner implications, and whether an opportunity should be pursued, watched, or passed.

## Media systems

Signalproof media research includes human-controlled AI assistance for voice, audio, video, transcription, editing, production workflows, provenance, and quality assurance.

The aim is not to remove the producer. It is to increase production capability while retaining authorship, performance judgment, editorial authority, and recovery.

## Agent and capability research

Signalproof evaluates external AI agents, harnesses, models, skills, and tools before deciding whether to adopt, adapt, integrate, isolate, block, or reject them.

This work recognizes that powerful agent systems can create real operational risk when users do not understand permissions, execution scope, external dependencies, memory behavior, or recovery boundaries.

## Research posture

Signalproof research favors:

- current evidence over hype;
- controlled testing over assumption;
- provenance over unsupported claims;
- reversible adoption over lock-in;
- modular capability over unnecessary dependency;
- human authority over opaque autonomy.
