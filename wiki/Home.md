## What this Wiki is

This is Signalproof's public maintained knowledge layer. Useful public knowledge should accumulate here rather than repeatedly disappearing into chats, one-off research sessions, or temporary working context.

The Wiki is still an explanatory and compiled-knowledge surface. It does **not** replace raw evidence, canonical repositories, release artifacts, governed registries, Build or Assurance Ledger evidence, runtime state, or product-specific authority.

For Signalproof Skills, the protected `main` branch, `SKILL-REGISTRY.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, and the individual active skill files remain authoritative for public skill-suite state.

## Knowledge model

Signalproof distinguishes among:

```text
RAW EVIDENCE
    ↓
COMPILED KNOWLEDGE
    ↓
DECISIONS / POLICY
    ↓
OPERATIONAL STATE
    ↓
AUTHORIZED USE
```

The public Wiki primarily contains compiled public knowledge and explanation. A page may point toward evidence or authority, but publishing prose does not create authority.

## Core thesis

AI capability is increasing faster than most individuals and organizations can responsibly absorb it. More models, agents, automations, integrations, and applications do not automatically create better outcomes.

Signalproof focuses on the human operating system around those capabilities: clarity, authority, boundaries, readiness, evidence, recovery, continuity, evaluation, accumulated knowledge, and deliberate adoption.

The objective is not maximum automation. The objective is **useful human-AI capability under meaningful human control**.

## Public boundary

Wiki knowledge is public by default when it is intentionally suitable for release. Private, customer-specific, security-sensitive, proprietary, credential-bearing, or restricted operational material remains in the private canonical source that owns it.

Public documentation should explain enough to be useful without weakening safety, privacy, ownership, or governance.
