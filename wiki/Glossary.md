# Glossary

## Agent
An AI-enabled software system that can select or execute actions toward a goal. Signalproof evaluates agents by authority, reach, dependencies, evidence, recoverability, and human control rather than by capability alone.

## Build Ledger
A continuity and evidence discipline for preserving significant development state, decisions, validation, rollback, lessons, and authorization for the next phase.

## Canonical state
The authoritative accepted state for a repository, artifact, record, or system. A branch, draft, mockup, or generated explanation is not canonical merely because it exists.

## Capability
A model, tool, skill, service, integration, agent, runtime, or other component that can add behavior to a human-AI system.

## Clarity
Understanding the real goal, present state, constraints, risks, and decision before increasing AI capability or automation.

## Compiled knowledge
Maintained summaries, concepts, entities, relationships, comparisons, contradictions, and conclusions derived from source material. Compiled knowledge is useful institutional memory but is not automatically policy or technical authority.

## Evidence class
A label describing what kind of support exists for a claim, such as artifact-backed fact, runtime-verified fact, human-observed fact, design authority, inference, or proposal.

## GO HAMM
A shorthand for the Human + AI Maturity Model: an approach to increasing AI capability through clarity, readiness, learning, correction, integration, automation, and governed scale.

## Governed learning
The process of converting a lesson from real work into reusable capability through evidence, testing, approval, versioning, and controlled activation.

## Human authority
The ability of a human operator or owner to meaningfully understand, bound, approve, stop, challenge, reverse, or redirect material system behavior.

## Human + AI Maturity
The degree to which human judgment, workflow clarity, technical readiness, evidence, governance, and recovery are prepared for the level of AI capability being used.

## Knowledge Plane
The persistent Signalproof layer that connects raw evidence, ingest and normalization, compiled knowledge, decisions and policy, operational state, and authorized use while preserving the authority boundary between them.

## Provenance
The traceable record of where a claim, artifact, decision, or knowledge item came from, how it was supported, when it was checked, and what system owns its authoritative state.

## Recovery
The ability to restore a trustworthy prior or known-good state and verify that restoration.

## Signalproof
A human-centered operating standard and developing ecosystem for using AI while preserving meaningful human control, evidence integrity, continuity, decision integrity, recovery, and proof.

## Signalproof Skills
The public governed suite of reusable operating disciplines maintained in the `docreo/Signalproof-Skills` repository.

## Verification
The act of determining whether a specific claim is actually supported by appropriate evidence.

## Wiki
The maintained public knowledge projection for Doc Reo and Signalproof. It accumulates safe public knowledge and explanation but does not supersede canonical repositories, evidence, policy, ledgers, runtime state, or release authority.

## Writeback
The controlled process of taking durable knowledge produced by research, analysis, development, or support work and updating the maintained knowledge layer so that future work can reuse it.
