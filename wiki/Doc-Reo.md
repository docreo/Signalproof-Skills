# About Doc Reo

**Doc Reo** is the professional identity of Mareo-Ahmir Lawson, an author, producer, educator, speaker, U.S. Army Gulf War veteran, and human-AI systems builder.

His work sits at the intersection of media, learning design, software development, artificial intelligence, business systems, and human-centered technology.

## Current focus

Doc Reo's current work centers on one question:

> How can people gain the benefits of increasingly capable AI while retaining meaningful human authority, judgment, continuity, accountability, recovery, and ownership?

That question drives the development of **Signalproof**, the Human + AI Maturity Model, governed AI-assisted build methods, local-first AI infrastructure, readiness tools, media systems, research programs, and educational material.

## Working philosophy

The recurring principle across Doc Reo's work is that technology should increase human capability without quietly erasing human authority.

That means asking more than whether an AI system *can* perform an action. It also means asking:

- Should it perform the action?
- Who authorized it?
- What evidence supports the decision?
- What does it have access to?
- What happens when it fails?
- Can the human understand and reverse the result?
- What should remain human-controlled?
