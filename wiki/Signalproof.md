# What Is Signalproof?

**Signalproof** is a human-centered operating standard for working with artificial intelligence while preserving meaningful human control, evidence integrity, decision integrity, continuity, recovery, and proof.

Signalproof is not one model, one agent, one application, or one automation platform. It is the control and operating discipline around those technologies.

## The problem Signalproof addresses

The AI market rewards speed, novelty, integration, and automation. Those can be valuable, but they also create recurring failure modes:

- adopting tools before understanding their permissions or dependencies;
- automating unclear or broken processes;
- treating model output as evidence;
- losing decisions and context across tools or conversations;
- allowing agents to act beyond the human operator's intended authority;
- replacing working systems without preserving rollback;
- confusing a successful demonstration with production readiness;
- accumulating applications faster than organizations can govern them.

Signalproof treats these as operating-system problems, not merely software-selection problems.

## Core principles

Signalproof public work currently emphasizes these principles:

1. Human authority remains meaningful.
2. Inspect before rebuilding.
3. Protect what already works.
4. Change the smallest necessary surface.
5. Separate evidence from inference.
6. Verify before declaring success.
7. Preserve recovery.
8. Stop unproductive loops.
9. Leave proof.
10. Turn solved problems into future capability.
11. Close meaningful milestones before opening the next development phase.
12. Preserve project state before continuity becomes a risk.

## Signalproof is both a framework and an ecosystem

Signalproof is developing across several connected layers:

- **Operating standard** — principles for human-controlled AI use.
- **Skills** — reusable governed operating disciplines for AI-assisted work.
- **Readiness and maturity** — ways to assess whether a person, workflow, system, or organization is prepared for deeper AI use.
- **Governance** — evidence classes, approval boundaries, change control, recovery, and release discipline.
- **Software** — tools that embody those operating principles.
- **Research and education** — white papers, public explanations, experiments, and practical guidance.

## The goal

The goal is not to slow AI adoption for its own sake.

The goal is to make adoption **deliberate, inspectable, recoverable, and useful**.

A Signalproof system should help a human know what is happening, why it is happening, what evidence supports the state, what authority the AI has, what remains uncertain, and how to recover when something goes wrong.
